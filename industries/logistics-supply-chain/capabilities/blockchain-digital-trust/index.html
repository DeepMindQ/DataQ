Blockchain & Digital Trust for Logistics & Supply Chain

Why this capability matters here#

In Logistics & Supply Chain, blockchain & digital trust delivers distributed-ledger programs where a shared, verifiable record has economic value: enterprise dlt networks for multi-party workflows, asset tokenization, smart contract engineering and audit, provenance and traceability, decentralized identity with verifiable credentials, and on-chain analytics for compliance. That is the baseline pattern; the differentiator is what it connects to: enterprise dlt network engineering, asset tokenization & digital assets, smart contract engineering & audit — delivered on Dataq Ledger and Dataq Sentinel with the sector's regulatory posture built in.

The pressure points we attack#

  • Compliance-grade provenance missing

    EUDR-grade due diligence and custody evidence for high-value goods remain paper-based; customs and buyers escalate.

  • Visibility stitched from portals

    Carrier portals, EDI and telematics produce a patchwork; the control tower reads yesterday. Exceptions surface as customer complaints.

  • Dispatch and exception work manual

    Planners re-plan routes and loads by hand when trucks break down or orders change; exception queues eat capacity that should improve the network.

How we deliver it in this sector#

The delivery pattern composes 6 services from the Blockchain & Digital Trust tower — Enterprise DLT Network Engineering, Asset Tokenization & Digital Assets, Smart Contract Engineering & Audit, Provenance, Traceability & Supply Chain Trust as the usual core — sequenced through the Dataq Method with sector controls: CMR/ADR/IMDG transport rules obligations shape the design from the first sprint.

Services we deploy in this sector#

  • Enterprise DLT Network Engineering

    Permissioned networks for multi-party truth: governance, consensus and integration done institutionally.

  • Asset Tokenization & Digital Assets

    Instruments as tokens: issuance, custody and lifecycle under securities-law discipline.

  • Smart Contract Engineering & Audit

    Contracts as production software: specification, formal verification and audit before value moves.

  • Provenance, Traceability & Supply Chain Trust

    Proof, not paperwork: EUDR, DPP and chain-of-custody systems regulators and customers can verify.

Proof patterns in this industry#

  • EUDR chain-of-custody

    Anchored custody events for in-scope commodities with regulator interfaces (due-diligence evidence in hours)

  • High-value custody network

    Shared evidence between shipper, carrier and insurer (claims and disputes collapse)

  • Empty miles

    Down 10-20% with dynamic matching

  • Detention and dwell time

    Down 25-40% with yard/dock orchestration

  • Exception resolution time

    Hours instead of days via agentic remediation

    Governance note#

    Two regimes anchor the control design here: CMR/ADR/IMDG transport rules and EUDR. Deforestation due diligence for in-scope commodities with evidence duties. Evidence for both is generated by the delivery itself — control tests, replay trails and documentation are artifacts of the program, not a reconstruction project.

    How a delivery runs#

    The engagement cadence below is the standard pattern for this capability. Weeks 1–2 lock the baseline: the current metric is measured with your team and signed before build starts. Weeks 3–8 deliver the pilot on bounded scope — one decision path, one workflow, one plant or one portfolio — with a control owner named on your side. Weeks 9–12 industrialize what worked: runbooks, monitoring, enablement and a measured delta against the signed baseline. Anything that misses its gate is stopped or redirected with a documented decision, not quietly extended.

    What you receive#

    Four named artifacts are delivered in the first quarter of every program of this type:

    • Baseline diagnostic

      The signed measurement of where the metric stands today, with the method written out so the number can be defended to finance.

    • Decision and control map

      Every automated decision path, its policy checks, its human checkpoint and its owner on one page.

    • Evidence pack

      Logs, evaluations and approvals collected automatically as the program runs — ready for internal audit or a regulator.

    • Value ledger

      A running record of the delta against baseline, updated at every gate, reviewed at every steering meeting.
