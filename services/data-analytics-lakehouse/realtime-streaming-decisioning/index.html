Real-Time Streaming & Event-Driven Decisioning

The honest pitch for real-time streaming & event-driven decisioning fits in a sentence — Decisions at event speed — sub-second pipelines from sensor, click or transaction to action. — and the rest of this page is the how: measured baseline, bounded build, evidence in flight.

ServicesData, Analytics & LakehouseReal-Time Streaming & Event-Driven Decisioning
Book an Executive Briefing Request a Value Assessment

The job this service does#

Batch thinking caps business performance: fraud caught after settlement, stockouts discovered at replenishment, churn addressed after the customer calls to leave. We build streaming backbones — Kafka or managed equivalents, Flink/Spark Structured Streaming processing, exactly-once semantics where money moves — feeding online feature stores and decision APIs that act within the customer moment, the machine cycle or the fraud window.

Engineering discipline makes it trustworthy: schema-first topics, watermark handling for late events, back-pressure design, and chaos drills that prove recovery. We pair the stack with event-storming workshops so the topics and events reflect how the business actually works — the difference between a streaming platform and a streaming mess.

Why this matters here#

Programs of Real-Time Streaming & Event-Driven Decisioning run in Banking & Financial Services, Retail & Consumer Goods, Telecom, Media & Entertainment, Logistics & Supply Chain — always against one named number, always with the baseline signed first.

The system, piece by piece#

What ships, in the order it ships:

  • Streaming backbone

    Kafka-class ingestion with schema registry, replay and topic governance

  • Stream processing

    Flink/Spark streaming with exactly-once semantics and watermark design

  • Online feature store

    point-in-time-correct features serving decision APIs at p99 under 150 ms

  • Event-storming alignment

    domain-derived event models with business ownership

  • Resilience drills

    chaos testing for broker loss, late data and consumer lag

Deliverables, without dressing them up#

Named artifacts, handed to named owners — Real-Time Streaming & Event-Driven Decisioning edition:

  • Production streaming backbone with governed topics and SLAs

    Versioned, signed, and formatted for the people who will audit it.

  • Real-time decision path from event to action with latency budget

    Produced as the build runs, not reconstructed afterwards.

  • Online/offline feature parity for model serving

    Handed to a named owner on your side, with a working session included.

  • Operational dashboards: lag, throughput, decision latency

    Versioned, signed, and formatted for the people who will audit it.

The stack, chosen for operability#

Real-Time Streaming & Event-Driven Decisioning is built against defined standards: Kafka, Flink, Spark Structured Streaming, Redis, Feast, Kubernetes. Each choice is justified in writing as part of the architecture baseline, so your team can defend it without us in the room.

How we run the build#

  • Weeks 1–2 — baseline

    Real-Time Streaming & Event-Driven Decisioning starts with the measurement: the metric, the owner, the current number, signed.

  • Weeks 3–8 — bounded build

    One working slice on your estate, streaming backbone first, gates written with your team.

  • Weeks 9–13 — evidence and decision

    The Real-Time Streaming & Event-Driven Decisioning gate review: baseline versus slice, in the metric's own units, plus a scale recommendation your board can audit.
