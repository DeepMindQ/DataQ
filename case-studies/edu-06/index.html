An online university

Admissions operations on governed agents — application processing time down 66% and counselor hours returned to recruitment

Education & ResearchAgentic AI & HyperautomationGlobal Identity withheld · Baseline audited · Method under NDA

What we walked into#

Identity withheld — an online university whose admissions funnel processed 40,000 applications a term with counselors doing data entry between recruitment trips. Figures are the client's own, shared with permission.

What the baseline showed#

The baseline term timed the funnel: 9.4 days median application-to-decision, 71% of counselor hours on transcript verification and data entry, and enrollment yield leaking at every waiting week.

Verification rules existed but lived in training docs; execution depended on which counselor read which page. The rules were the product; the application was just its paperwork.

What went into production#

We moved admissions processing to governed agents: transcripts parsed and verified against the codified rules with confidence per field, exceptions routed to counselors with the evidence attached, decision packets assembled automatically, and the rules library versioned under the registrar's ownership. Counselors review decisions, not documents.

The measured delta#

Transfer notes#

The codified-rules pattern transfers to any verification workflow; the rules were this university's admissions policy. Replication starts from its training docs — the diagnostic turns them into the first library.

Figures on this page are the client's, reproduced with permission. Method notes and measurement designs are available under NDA.
