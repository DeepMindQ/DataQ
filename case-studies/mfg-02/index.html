Case study

A specialty chemicals producer

Yield +3.4 points and energy per tonne down 17% — APC augmentation program

Manufacturing & IndustrialEU/US

The situation

A specialty chemicals producer, Manufacturing & Industrial sector, outcome-linked engagement, blended delivery team. Figures are the client's own, shared with permission.

What the baseline showed

APC setpoints static while feedstock varied; yield and energy losses accepted as normal variance.

Critical rotating assets uninstrumented; failures unscheduled and costly. Batch investigations took weeks across disjoint quality/ERP records.

The system we shipped

ML-augmented control with constraint awareness now adapts setpoints to feedstock drift; rotating equipment carries vibration/thermal telemetry into RUL models wired to CMMS; the batch genealogy platform unified process, quality and ERP with lineage — investigations now close in hours.

Outcomes, as the client reported them

Why it worked

  • Applied AI & GenAI

    the models and retrieval systems were evaluated before anyone trusted them — grounded answers, cited sources, measured quality.

  • IoT, Twin & Robotics

    the physical layer stayed synchronized with reality, so the decisions made on top of it were trustworthy.

  • Data & Lakehouse

    one governed definition per metric ended the metric disputes before the build scaled.

If you are considering the same pattern

If the specialty chemicals producer program reads like your own estate, the honest advice is: start with the baseline diagnostic, not with the build.
