Global Offices

Four delivery centers, one accountability chain — strategy in the US, execution around the sun.

About UsGlobal Offices
Book an Executive Briefing Request a Value Assessment

Dataq Software runs four centers with distinct mandates. Programs are staffed by named pods that stay with the outcome — the team that designs your decision hub is accountable for its production results, wherever they sit.

The centers#

Operating rhythm#

Follow-the-sun operations for managed SOC, platform operations and agent-fleet operations; capability centers of excellence per tower; and a rotation program that moves engineers across centers so patterns travel with people.

What each center carries#

  • US — Tysons Corner

    Quantum and advanced compute practice, board advisory, commercial models tied to outcomes.

  • UK — London

    DORA and regulatory programs, financial-services delivery, European data-residency patterns.

  • India — Global Execution Core

    Data and platform factories, agentic engineering pods, 24/7 operations depth.

  • KSA — Riyadh

    Vision 2030 programs, sovereign delivery models, energy and smart-city practices.

Security zoning across centers#

Delivery pods operate under a single security architecture: zero-exposure defaults, customer-managed keys for client data, zoned networks per center, and cross-center access governed by the same zero-trust controls we sell. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 cover the operated estate end to end.
