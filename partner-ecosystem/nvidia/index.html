NVIDIA Alliance

The Dataq Software alliance with NVIDIA: what is certified, how joint deliveries run, and the scenarios this page can be held to.

About UsPartner EcosystemNVIDIA
Book an Executive Briefing Request a Value Assessment

What the partnership covers#

The alliance with NVIDIA covers Accelerated computing for AI factories and inference at scale — backed by joint reference architectures, certification on both sides, and a support map with real names in it.

Reference architecture#

The architecture for NVIDIA Alliance is deliberately auditable: every stage exposes its inputs, its owner and its failure policy, and the decision path shares a timeline with the audit path. Nothing important happens off the record.

Reference scenarios we deliver#

  • AI inference factories with token-level cost governance

    joint delivery scenario — reference architecture, certified team and support path in place

  • End-to-end accelerated RAG and multimodal platforms

    joint delivery scenario — reference architecture, certified team and support path in place

  • GPU capacity planning, scheduling and right-sizing programs

    joint delivery scenario — reference architecture, certified team and support path in place

  • Omniverse-class simulation pipelines for digital twins

    joint delivery scenario — reference architecture, certified team and support path in place

Engineering standards & stack#

Standards we hold the build to, component by component:

  • Gpu Pools Sized

    GPU capacity matched to measured demand, with queueing that makes contention visible.

  • Nim Microservices

    Model serving as managed containers — versioned, monitored, rollback-tested.

  • Nemo

    Guardrailed LLM tuning and serving, with evaluation wired before deployment.

  • Cuda-Accelerated Feature Engineering Where It Pays

    GPU time spent only where the feature math actually bottlenecks the pipeline.

  • Mig Partitioning

    Instance right-sizing with migration plans — the quiet, unglamorous part of cloud cost control.

Integration surface#

  • dbt transformation graph

    Tested, documented models with lineage exposure — the transform layer your analysts can actually read.

  • data contracts

    Typed schemas with owners and freshness expectations, agreed before a pipeline is allowed to ship.

  • Delta / Iceberg tables

    Open table formats underneath everything we build, so your data never belongs to a vendor.

  • Unity Catalog governance

    A single governance plane over tables, models and notebooks — access your auditors can replay.

How a joint engagement runs#

The joint pattern for NVIDIA Alliance: our pod does the build, your named owners hold the gates, and the evidence is generated in systems your teams already operate.

What you receive#

The quarter-close package, built to be handed over rather than demonstrated:

  • Joint architecture pack

    The target architecture drawn against your estate, with interfaces and owners made explicit.

  • Certified delivery pod

    A named team with the clearances your estate requires — the same people from diagnosis to handover.

  • Support map

    Who answers when something breaks — named roles, response expectations and the escalation path.

Ask for the NVIDIA reference pack — or bring your use case to a joint working session with both platform teams.
